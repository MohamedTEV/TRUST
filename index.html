Classifying remote sensing archives without a single label

Vision-language models can name the scenes of an archive from the class names alone, yet on remote sensing imagery their zero-shot predictions are strongly biased: a few classes absorb most of the predictions. TRUST classifies the whole archive at once, removes this bias, and brings vision foundation models into zero-shot classification of remote sensing scenes.

+27.5points over zero-shot CLIP, on average over 12 benchmarks
+8.5points over the most recent state of the art (CVPR 2026)

0labels used, on more than 166,000 images
